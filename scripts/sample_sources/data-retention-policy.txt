DATA RETENTION POLICY FOR MACHINE LEARNING
Lumenfield Labs, Data Governance office. Version 3.

1. Purpose

This policy defines how long the data used and produced by machine learning systems is kept,
and how it is deleted.

2. Training data

Raw training data is retained for 24 months after collection. After 24 months it is deleted
from all storage locations, including backups, within the following 30 days.

Personal data is pseudonymized before it is used for training. The pseudonymization keys are
stored separately and are accessible only to the Data Governance office.

3. Inference logs

Inference logs, which contain model inputs and predictions, are retained for 90 days. After
90 days they are aggregated into daily statistics and the individual records are deleted.

4. Model artifacts

Model artifacts and their training metadata are kept in the model registry for 3 years
after the model is retired, so that past predictions can be explained.

5. Deletion requests

Requests from a person to delete their data are processed within 30 days. The person is
informed when the deletion is complete.
